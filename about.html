04 · About

Deal discipline, operator range.

I'm a corporate development and strategic finance professional with more than $500M in executed M&A, capital raises and strategic transactions across technology, media, telecom and AI infrastructure. I've led deals end-to-end, from origination and modeling through diligence, negotiation and close, for clients including Citi, IFC (World Bank), CDC Group and SoftBank during my years in TMT investment banking at Detecon (Deutsche Telekom Group).

I've also sat on the other side of the table: building corporate finance as VP at Meta Mind Global, shaping strategy at Chevron, starting as an M&A analyst at Citi, and today driving growth and operations at Cloud Destinations. Alongside the operating roles, I'm a startup mentor at Plug and Play and a strategic advisor to Runopt (AI for property development) and HiveBotics (robotics), helping founders pressure-test business models, sharpen positioning and get investor-ready. My edge is classical deal discipline paired with AI-accelerated workflows. Everything on this site was built the way I run live transaction analysis.

In practice, working AI-native means Claude agents with MCP integrations handle diligence document review, market and competitor research, and data extraction from SEC filings; API pipelines keep rates and trading multiples current; and automated recalculation and tie-out checks validate every model change. Investment committee materials, budget-variance reads, and board memos come together in days instead of weeks, with the audit trail intact.
